### 1. Scope and purpose

This draft applies only to original Compendlore material in this SDK repository. Darkadia Studio is
the project operator; its complete legal identity must be inserted before adoption. Third-party
dependencies and incorporated material remain subject to their own licenses and notices.

The SDK is an extraction of the interfaces and elements needed to build addons compatible with
official Compendlore CMS versions. It is updated when CMS engine changes require it. It does not
transfer rights in the CMS implementation.

### 2. SDK development permission

Subject to this draft, a recipient may read and study the SDK, use it to develop and test an
independent addon, modify it privately, create a personal fork, and share changes reasonably
necessary for an authorized contribution to the official SDK project.

An independent addon remains owned by its creator or other applicable rights holder. No ownership of
an independent addon is transferred to Darkadia Studio merely because the addon uses the SDK or is
compatible with Compendlore.

A creator may distribute an independent addon that calls, references, or depends on the official
SDK. The creator may include only those SDK elements technically necessary in the distributed addon
when the official packaging format requires inclusion, provided all applicable copyright,
attribution, and third-party notices are preserved. This targeted permission does not authorize
redistribution of the SDK as a standalone substitute, of the CMS, or of a modified Compendlore
distribution. The exact boundary of incorporated SDK elements requires legal review before
activation.

### 3. General permissions and services

A recipient may run the SDK privately, use a personal fork for development, and use an unmodified
official SDK release on infrastructure of their choice. Separate development, integration, hosting,
support, and maintenance services may be charged for; no Compendlore license may be sold or resold
through those services.

### 4. Restrictions

Without separate written permission, a recipient may not:

- sell or sublicense the SDK as a standalone product;
- redistribute the SDK or CMS as an unofficial or modified distribution;
- use the contribution-fork permission as a general redistribution right;
- present an addon, fork, or build as officially endorsed when it is not;
- use Compendlore names, logos, release marks, or signing identities misleadingly.

Public forks and pull requests may be used only as reasonably necessary for the authorized
contribution workflow. Legal review must confirm compatibility with the selected platform’s terms.

### 5. Conditions and no warranty

Copyright, license, attribution, and third-party notices must be preserved. Contributions are
governed separately by CONTRIBUTING.md and, only if adopted later, the applicable CLA.md. No
historical contribution is automatically covered by a future CLA.

Ce projet s’applique uniquement aux éléments originaux de Compendlore présents dans ce dépôt SDK.

Sous réserve de ce projet, un destinataire peut lire et étudier le SDK, l’utiliser pour développer
